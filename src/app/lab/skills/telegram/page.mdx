**Depth: shipping.** Most things I build end up inside Telegram. Not a strategy
— it's just where people already are. Ask someone here to install an app and
you've lost half of them; send a bot link in a group and you haven't.

What I've shipped into it: [shoshshi](/lab/ideas/shoshshi) (Mini App + grammY
bot, phone gate, admin, broadcast), a downloader bot on yt-dlp, a tiny bot that
posts one dhikr a day and has run untouched for months, chat verification for a
streaming room, resume delivery for nerd.uz because "send it to me on Telegram"
is what people actually ask for.

## What it gives you

- **Identity, free.** `initData` is signed — validate it server-side and you
  have a real user. No signup form, no password reset, no email deliverability.
- **Sharing that works.** A `t.me` link opens the thing. No store review, no
  install prompt.
- **Local payment rails.** Payme, Click, Uzum — and no 15–30% store cut, which
  changes which prices are even possible.
- **A notification channel you own.** Rarest item on the list.

## What it takes back

- Rate limits shape your UX. A progress bar becomes "edit one message every
  three seconds".
- Captions cap at 1024 characters, so the design becomes caption + follow-ups.
- Ten photos sent in a loop is ten notifications; send a media group.
- The web platform isn't your browser — viewport quirks, a theme you must
  respect, a back button whose meaning you inherit, and whatever WebView the
  phone shipped with.

## One small thing

Two days into a Mini App on React 19, `npm install` refused: `@twa-dev/sdk`
pinned `react@^18.3.1` as a peer. I didn't want `--legacy-peer-deps` in a
project's install instructions, so I filed
[twa-dev/SDK#47](https://github.com/twa-dev/SDK/issues/47) instead of working
around it quietly. Closed now.

Telegram tooling outside the Russian-speaking core is thin. The gap you hit is
usually the gap everyone hits.

Still learning: payments end to end with real refunds, webhooks at a size where
polling stops being fine, and keeping bot state sane once "just a bot" has
become an application.
