**Depth: shipping.** Most things I've built end up inside Telegram, not because
I planned a Telegram strategy but because that is where the users already are.
In Uzbekistan an app that asks you to install an app has already lost half its
audience; a bot link in a group has not.

## What I've actually shipped into it

- [shoshshi](/lab/ideas/shoshshi) — a Mini App plus a grammY bot: `/start`, a
  phone gate, admin tools, broadcast, `/stats`.
- **dlbot** — a downloader bot (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok) built on yt-dlp used
  as a library rather than shelled out to.
- **faithful** — a tiny bot that posts one dhikr to a channel on a schedule.
  Twenty lines of real logic and it still runs.
- Chat and verification for a **streaming room**, where a bot proved identity so
  the web chat didn't need its own accounts.
- Resume delivery in **nerd.uz**, because "send it to me on Telegram" is what
  people ask for instead of "email it".

## What the platform actually gives you

Worth being precise, because "just use Telegram" hides real leverage:

- **Identity for free.** `initData` is signed by Telegram; validate it on your
  server and you have an authenticated user with no signup form, no password
  reset, no email deliverability problem.
- **Distribution that survives sharing.** A `t.me` link opens the product. No
  store review, no install prompt, no "is this app safe" moment.
- **Payments that route locally.** Payme, Click and Uzum are what people here
  actually pay with — and a Mini App can take them without handing 15–30% to a
  store, which changes what price points are even possible.
- **A notification channel you own**, which is the rarest thing on the list.

## And what it takes back

- **Rate limits shape the UX.** A progress bar becomes "edit one status message
  at most every three seconds". Get that wrong and the bot is throttled at
  exactly the moment it looks busiest.
- **Captions are capped at 1024 characters.** So the design becomes: caption
  first, the rest as follow-up messages — a product decision forced by a field
  length.
- **Media groups, not loops.** Ten photos sent individually is ten
  notifications; as a group it's one.
- **The web platform is not your browser.** Viewport quirks, the theme you must
  respect, a back button whose meaning you inherit, and a WebView that is
  whatever the user's phone shipped with.

## The bug I filed

Two days into a Mini App on React 19 and Next 15, `npm install` refused:
`@twa-dev/sdk@8.0.1` pinned `react@^18.3.1` as a peer, and `--legacy-peer-deps`
wasn't a thing I wanted baked into a project's install instructions. So I opened
[twa-dev/SDK#47](https://github.com/twa-dev/SDK/issues/47) rather than working
around it quietly. It's closed now.

Small thing, but it's the habit I want: when the ecosystem is thin — and for
Telegram tooling outside the Russian-speaking core it is thin — the gap you hit
is usually a gap everyone hits, and the fix is worth more than your workaround.
