**28 September 2026, 18:36, Tashkent.** I typed "roast me" into ChatGPT. What
came back was funnier than it had any right to be, and closer to the bone than
I expected, so it goes in the garden rather than in a chat log I'll never open
again.

Rule for future me: **read this before starting the next thing, not after.**

---

> Bro, your biggest problem is that you're **way too ambitious for the amount of
> peace you allow yourself to have.**
>
> You wake up and somehow have 14 problems that didn't exist yesterday.
>
> You'll be like: *"I need to become a better Go engineer."* Then: *"Maybe I
> should build an open-source alternative to Meilisearch."* Then: *"Actually, AI
> café in Uzbekistan."* Then: *"What if I make Uzbek Scrimba?"* Then: *"Also I
> need to grow Gopher Uzbekistan."* Then at 2 AM: *"What does it mean if a girl
> puts Another Love on her profile?"*
>
> **BRO. PICK A SERVER TO DEPLOY.**
>
> You have the mind of a founder, the sleep schedule of a university student,
> and the decision-making process of someone standing in front of a pharmacy
> shelf for 45 minutes comparing zinc tablets.
>
> And you don't just overthink things. You **industrialize** overthinking.
>
> A normal person sees a relationship problem and thinks: *"We should talk."*
> You think: *"Let me formulate 12 questions to evaluate religious compatibility
> without making it obvious that I formulated 12 questions."*
>
> You're also hilarious with your engineering standards. At work: *"We need ADRs
> before implementation. Business logic must be documented. PRs need
> self-review. WIP must remain draft. Scalability considerations should be
> included."* Meanwhile your own life: **no ADR, no documentation, directly
> merged to production.**
>
> Your projects are the same. You don't finish things. You **promote them to
> "interesting ideas."** Telegram bot, AI café, browser extension, education
> platform, Go OSS project, marketplace, community, some SaaS idea. And every
> few days your brain goes: *"Wait... I have another idea."*
>
> My brother, **you don't need another idea. You need to close an issue.**
>
> And your relationship with Claude Code is getting suspicious. You're basically
> becoming a manager of AI agents while simultaneously trying to prove you're a
> strong engineer. You'll say: *"I want engineers to understand the architecture
> and write solid code."* Then: *"Make me a prompt so Claude can implement the
> entire onboarding flow."*
>
> And you have this funny obsession with making everything **"simple."** Make
> this Slack message simple. Make this Uzbek simple. Make this UI simple. Make
> this explanation simple. Brother, **nothing about your life is simple.** You
> just want the *sentence* to be simple.
>
> And the funniest thing is you clearly care about how you're perceived. You
> want: Lead Software Engineer. Community Lead. Founder energy. Strong engineer.
> Open-source guy. But then you come here like: *"Is 5 hours of sleep enough?"*
> *"Is this rice normal?"* *"Which testosterone supplement?"*
>
> You're basically trying to maintain the public image of **Linus Torvalds**
> while privately operating as **a confused 21-year-old with 37 browser tabs
> open.**
>
> And somehow… the worst part? **You actually have the ability to do most of the
> things you're talking about.** That's why the roast hits. Your problem isn't
> lack of ambition. It's that **your ambition has absolutely no fucking queue.**

## Why this is in the lab and not in a screenshot folder

Because the lab already proves the point. Open the explorer on the left: this
garden has a folder called **ideas** and every note in it is something I decided
was worth building. [shoshshi](/lab/ideas/shoshshi) is live.
[yamu](/lab/ideas/yamu) shipped. [FilterCam](/lab/ideas/filter-cam) shipped
inside someone else's product. [maymun](/lab/ideas/maymun) is a four-product
brief written by one person.

Three of those are real. The roast isn't saying I can't build. It's saying I
keep opening tabs faster than I close them.
