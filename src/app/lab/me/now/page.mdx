A [/now page](https://nownownow.com/about). Not a plan — what actually has my
attention. Everything not on it is waiting, and saying that in public is the
point.

**Updated 12 November 2026.**

**Work.** Lead Software Engineer at [dona.uz](https://dona.uz), a live-shopping
marketplace. Mobile app, seller panel, backend — which is where I'm learning
[Go](/lab/skills/golang) for real rather than from exercises.

**Building — two things that started as audience and now earn.**

[Botxona](/lab/ideas/botxona) ([botxona.uz](https://botxona.uz)): Telegram bot
hosting in so'm, live since the end of September.
[The first month](/lab/ideas/botxona/november-1) brought 96 users, 71 bots and
the first paying customers, with nothing spent on ads, because most people find
it by asking ChatGPT. So the docs are the marketing, and keeping them true is
part of the job. A few customers also asked me to build their bot, not just host
it. What matters this month is the first renewals at the end of November: one
payment is a try, a second one is a customer.

[Slaydxona](/lab/ideas/slaydxona) ([umbra.uz](https://umbra.uz)): AI
presentations for students. Honestly an audience machine: the first deck is free
once you join my channel. Over a thousand students have used it, and the paid
decks cover the AI bill with some left over. Lately: the new Uzbek Latin
alphabet and Karakalpak. A marketplace inside it is built and not shipped yet.

Both exist to grow the channel, so the next thing I ship already has people to
show it to.

**On the side.** [Rondo](https://t.me/rondogg_bot), small games inside
Telegram. One of them, [Timber](/timber), is playable right here on the site.

**Running, not moving.** [shoshshi](/lab/ideas/shoshshi) is live but untouched
since September; the cold-start problem is the same as it was.
[yamu](/lab/ideas/yamu): maintenance only.

**Waiting, and honestly waiting.** [maymun](/lab/ideas/maymun) — no product code
until the Uzbek voice passes a blind test with ten native speakers. That gate
*is* the work. [Oris](/lab/ideas/oris) is behind it.

**Learning.** [Arabic script](/lab/arabic), from letters to reading.

**Community.** Gopher Uzbekistan under [FLOSS Uzbekistan](https://floss.uz) —
[gopher.uz](https://gopher.uz) and the Telegram group.

The rule I was testing in September: one thing in front of the queue, everything
else written down and waiting. It didn't hold. There are two products in front
now, plus bots built for customers. What's different from what
[the roast](/lab/me/roast) warned about is that both of them earn, and both feed
the same channel. New idea → still a note in `ideas/`, not a repo.
