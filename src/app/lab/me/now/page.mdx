A [/now page](https://nownownow.com/about). Not a plan — what actually has my
attention. Everything not on it is waiting, and saying that in public is the
point.

**Updated 24 November 2026.**

**Work.** Lead Software Engineer at [dona.uz](https://dona.uz), a live-shopping
marketplace. Mobile app, seller panel, backend — which is where I'm learning
[Go](/lab/skills/golang) for real rather than from exercises.

**New at the front: a [YouTube channel](/lab/youtube).** An Uzbek backend
course in Go, from zero to senior, for people who think AI already took their
future, plus LeetCode, mock interviews and news. No camera: every frame is
rendered from code. The hard part is the voice. I'm fine-tuning an open Uzbek
TTS model on the livestreams of Shaxzod aka
([@shaxzodlab](https://www.youtube.com/@shaxzodlab)), who said *"bemalol"* when
I asked. As of today, 209 hours of streams are cut down to 53 hours of clean
speech, and the training run is on free Kaggle GPUs. [The note](/lab/youtube)
has the whole story.

**Building — two things that started as audience and now earn.**

[Botxona](/lab/ideas/botxona) ([botxona.uz](https://botxona.uz)): Telegram bot
hosting in so'm, live since the end of September.
[The first month](/lab/ideas/botxona/november-1) brought 96 users, 71 bots and
the first paying customers, with nothing spent on ads, because most people find
it by asking ChatGPT. What matters this month is still the first renewals at the
end of November: one payment is a try, a second one is a customer.

The last seven days went into one idea, *bots you can buy, not just host*
(about 120 commits):

- **Bot bozori**, a bot market. Every bot for sale comes with a two-phone
  Telegram simulator, so you try it before you pay. The Mini App inside the
  simulator is the real app running on demo data.
- **An open marketplace**, live since 22 November: anyone can list a bot, with
  a 20% commission, a three-day hold and payouts.
- **The free catalog bots grew up.** A dozen of them (shop, channel posts,
  support inbox, contests, queues and more) got a Pro admin panel as a Mini App,
  plus live demos.
- **A booking bot in five real layouts**, not five colour swaps. I threw out
  the palette-only version, because a palette isn't a design.
- **A support inbox** in the platform bot: you write to
  [@botxona_uz_bot](https://t.me/botxona_uz_bot), and we answer with a Telegram
  reply.
- **The first promo video and the first broadcast.** 153 people got it; 20 had
  already blocked the bot. That second number is the useful one.
- **More answers for AI assistants**: fact pages and a fuller `llms.txt`,
  because that's where people find us.

[Slaydxona](/lab/ideas/slaydxona) ([umbra.uz](https://umbra.uz)): AI
presentations for students. Honestly an audience machine: the first deck is free
once you join my channel. Over a thousand students have used it, and the paid
decks cover the AI bill with some left over. Its stack has now been reused
once: a course-selling bot with a Mini App and an admin dashboard, built for a
marketing teacher and live since 22 November.

Both exist to grow the channel, so the next thing I ship already has people to
show it to. The YouTube channel is the same bet, made bigger.

**On the side.** [Rondo](https://t.me/rondogg_bot), small games inside

**Learning.** [Arabic script](/lab/arabic), from letters to reading.

else written down and waiting. It didn't hold. There are now two products in front,
bots built for customers, and a channel being built from scratch. What's different
from what [the roast](/lab/me/roast) warned about is that the products earn, and
everything feeds the same audience. New idea → still a note in `ideas/`, not a repo.
