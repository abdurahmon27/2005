This is my **lab** — not a blog. A blog post is finished; these notes are not. I
keep them the way I keep a workshop: things half-built on the bench, labels on
the drawers, and a map on the wall so I can find my way back.

Three drawers:

- **[Skills](/lab/skills)** — what I can actually do, and how deep it really goes.
- **[Ideas](/lab/ideas)** — projects that exist in my head, some of them already in code.
- **[Me](/lab/me)** — who is writing all of this.

Use the explorer on the left to walk the tree, the graph on the right to see how
notes connect. Every dotted link is a note; every `#tag` is a node too.

## How this works

Each note is a plain MDX file under `src/app/lab/`, with `lab: true` in its
frontmatter. Folders become folders in the explorer, any link pointing at another
`/lab/...` note becomes an edge in the graph, and every tag becomes a node of its
own. Nothing is fetched at runtime — the whole garden is read off disk at build
time.
