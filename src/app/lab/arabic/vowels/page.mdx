Arabic writes the **consonants**. The short vowels are tiny marks — a stroke
above, a stroke below, a little curl — and most newspapers leave them out
entirely, because native readers don't need them.

Learners do, and so does any carefully written text: children's books,
poetry, dictionaries and classical texts are fully vowelled. Learn these
marks and every letter you met in stage 1 starts to speak.
