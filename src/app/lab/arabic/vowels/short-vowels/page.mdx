Three marks, three sounds. Each sits on the letter it follows:

- **fatḥa** — a small stroke **above**: <Syl l="ba" k="a" /> — *a*
- **kasra** — a small stroke **below**: <Syl l="ba" k="i" /> — *i*
- **ḍamma** — a small curl (a tiny wāw) **above**: <Syl l="ba" k="u" /> — *u*

Read the consonant and the vowel as one beat. Not "b… a", but *ba*.

## Heavy letters darken the vowel

After a heavy letter the *a* sounds deeper, almost like the *o* in "hot":
compare <Syl l="sin" /> and <Syl l="sad" />, <Syl l="ta" /> and
<Syl l="tta" />. Same mark, different colour of sound.
