Uzbek has thousands of words from Arabic. Some came through Persian, many
changed a vowel or two on the way (*kitāb → kitob*, *waqt → vaqt*), but you
can still hear them. That makes them perfect reading practice: once you
sound one out, you **recognise** it.

## The collection

Read each card before pressing it. The Uzbek word is underneath, if you need
it.

## The final test

No Latin, no sound. Read the word, then pick the Uzbek word it became. If you
can get through this one, you can read Arabic script.
