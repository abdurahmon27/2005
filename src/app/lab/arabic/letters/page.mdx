Arabic is written **right to left**, and the letters are joined like
handwriting. That sounds like two problems; it's really one shape per letter
that changes its ending depending on its neighbours.

Good news before we start: an Uzbek speaker already owns some of the "hard"
sounds — **x** (خ), **g‘** (غ) and **q** (ق) are in your mouth every day.
