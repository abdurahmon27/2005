## A letter's name is not its sound

The name of <Syl l="ba" k="name" /> is *bāʾ*, but on the page it just says
**b**. Learn the names so you can talk about letters; learn the sounds so you
can read. The row of syllables on each card — <Syl l="ba" k="a" />
<Syl l="ba" k="i" /> <Syl l="ba" k="u" /> — is what you'll actually say.

## Heavy and light

The orange dot marks the seven **heavy** letters: خ ص ض غ ط ق ظ. Say them
with the back of your tongue lifted, as if your mouth were full — the vowel
after them turns darker. Compare light <Syl l="sin" /> with heavy
<Syl l="sad" />, light <Syl l="ta" /> with heavy <Syl l="tta" />.

## How to learn them

- **Day 1–2:** names, in order. Press *hear all 28* and say them along.
- **Day 3–4:** sounds. Go card by card and repeat each syllable three times.
- **Day 5:** the quiz below, until you get 8 or more.
