I want to **read** Arabic script: look at a vowelled word and say it right.
Not speak the language, not translate — just read. That's a much smaller goal
than "learn Arabic", and it has a clear finish line.

An Uzbek speaker starts with an unfair advantage. Our language took thousands
of words from Arabic — *kitob, qalam, maktab, ilm, vaqt, xabar* — and three of
the "hard" sounds (**x**, **g‘**, **q**) are already in your mouth. This
course leans on both.

Everything here can be pressed and heard. Letters, syllables and words are
spoken by a neural Arabic voice, slowed down so the sound is easy to catch.

## How to walk it

1. **One lesson a day is enough.** The alphabet takes a week, not an afternoon.
2. **Listen before you read.** Press, repeat out loud, press again. Your ear
   trains your mouth.
3. **Do the quiz at the end of each lesson.** Under 7/10 means the lesson isn't
   done yet — that's fine.
4. **Hide the Latin as soon as you can.** Every grid has a button for it.

Progress is kept in your browser — press *mark as learned* at the end of a
lesson and the road above fills in.
